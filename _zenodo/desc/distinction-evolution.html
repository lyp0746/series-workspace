中文摘要。本著作把“统一”从愿望翻译成可检验的操作，并用一套以“区分”为起点的语言，把它推到可复算的定理：区分生成结构（拓扑、可测、连续），形式承载结构（一个对称双线性形式），硬核落在有限图上（两个临界耦合、两阶段相变、谱各向异性），再一路推到连续极限（第二个门槛归零、Weyl 密度）。

核心主张。统一有三级台阶——记号、定理、本体；本书只站前两级。第一级：区分（对象、属性、阈值、观察者）可以生成拓扑、σ-代数与连续模量；第二级：五个领域的相似度工具（协方差、Hessian、图拉普拉斯、内积、度量）满足同一组公理，由同一个二次型承载；第三级（本体统一）只提出一个候选，并明确它可能失败。

结构与证据。5 部分 25 章、259 页、369 节、30 幅插图、附录 A—F。第一部分把统一分成三级并给出七个识别赝品的问题；第二部分由区分生成空间、可测与连续（定理 7.1、7.2、8.1，命题 9.2）；第三部分给出形式与群（定理 11.1、11.2、12.1、12.3，命题 14.1）；第四部分是硬核；第五部分做覆盖、迁移与检验（36 领域 × 5 列，A 11／B 18／C 7）。

实证与算例。硬核部分全部可复算：临界耦合 β₁=α/λₙ、β₂=α/λ₂（环 C₁₀ 为 0.2500／2.6180，Petersen 为 0.2000／0.5000，完全图两值重合）；谱各向异性 R=λₙ/λ₂ 的九族闭式（网格 R=(8/π²)n²，n=40 时实测 1295.6 对闭式 1296.9）；连续极限三检验（λ₂/h²→π²、R∝n²、λₙ→8）；Weyl 计数与格点精确计数在 Λ≤320 上逐项相同；κ=½Var(λ) 一行求导即得；6 顶点同谱不同构的一对图（度序列 3,3,3,2,2,1 与 4,2,2,2,2,2）。附录 B 给出 6—12 点全部树（979 棵）的谱表与可运行代码。

诚实的学术纪律。30 条定理／命题／猜想逐条标级（A／甲 21、A／乙 5、B／乙 3、C／丙 1）；附录 D 是可证伪登记簿（append-only）：出局 4 条、成立 0 条、未执行 5 条——四条出局推翻的都是“越界读法”，没有一条推翻定理本身；并写明“出局多于成立”说明判据严，而不是结论少。

边界。硬核只对有限、无向、连通的图成立，有向图会让顺序反转（16.5 节反例）；连续侧没有 λₙ，两阶段结构塌缩为一个门槛；形式层面的统一不等于几何层面的同一（符号差不同，保形式群不同）；社会族 8 条全是 C 级，因为阈值本身是争议对象。

Abstract (English). This work turns “unification” from a wish into a testable operation, and pushes a distinction-based language all the way to recomputable theorems: distinctions generate structure (topology, measurability, continuity), a symmetric bilinear form carries that structure, the hard core sits on finite graphs (two critical couplings, a two-stage transition, spectral anisotropy), and it is then pushed to the continuum limit (the second threshold vanishes, a Weyl density takes over).

Core thesis. Unification has three grades — notation, theorem, ontology — and the book claims only the first two. Distinctions (object, attribute, threshold, observer) generate topology, σ-algebras and moduli of continuity; the similarity tools of five domains satisfy one set of axioms and are carried by one quadratic form; the third grade is offered only as a candidate, with its failure conditions stated.

Structure and evidence. Five parts, twenty-five chapters, 259 pages, 369 sections, 30 figures, appendices A–F. Part I grades unification and gives seven questions for spotting counterfeits; Part II generates space, measurability and continuity (Theorems 7.1, 7.2, 8.1); Part III gives forms and groups (Theorems 11.1, 11.2, 12.1, 12.3); Part IV is the hard core; Part V covers 36 domains across five columns (A 11 / B 18 / C 7), three migrations and six failure modes.

Evidence. Everything in the hard core is recomputable: the two critical couplings (C₁₀: 0.2500 / 2.6180; Petersen: 0.2000 / 0.5000; complete graph: both equal); closed forms for R=λₙ/λ₂ across nine families (grid R=(8/π²)n², 1295.6 measured vs 1296.9 at n=40); three continuum checks (λ₂/h²→π², R∝n², λₙ→8); Weyl counts equal to exact lattice counts for Λ≤320; κ=½Var(λ) by one differentiation; a Laplacian-cospectral non-isomorphic pair on six vertices. Appendix B lists the spectra of all trees on 6–12 vertices (979 trees) with runnable code.

Discipline. All thirty statements are graded (A 21, A/B 5, B 3, C 1). Appendix D is an append-only register: four refutations, zero confirmations, five unexecuted items — every refutation overturns an over-reading rather than a theorem, and the imbalance is stated as evidence of strict criteria rather than of few conclusions.

Boundaries. The hard core holds only for finite, undirected, connected graphs; directed graphs reverse the order (Section 16.5); on the continuum there is no λₙ, so the two-stage structure collapses into one threshold; formal sameness is not geometric sameness (different signature, different group); all eight social-science entries are grade C because the thresholds themselves are contested.

Features. Fully open source (content CC BY 4.0 / code MIT); three to five references per chapter; twelve data tables and thirty figures generated by scripts; Appendix E indexes every theorem and Appendix F gives a Chinese–English glossary. This edition rewrites the book from the distinction language and renames it The Language of Distinction.
