中文摘要。本书是这一系列(九本书、十四篇工作论文)的入口读本:用六个核心,把六本学术专著写成一本从十岁到专家都能读完的书。它不新增实证结论,只做一件事——把同一笔账单的六个项目,同一把尺子的六种用法,写成可读、可用、可自查的通俗语言。

核心主张。看得见的世界背后有一张看不见的账单,账单只有三个价格:看得准、拼得上、传得下去;紧预算下最多付得起两个。六个核心(看见、记住、跟上、选择、讲述、临界)不是六个并列的概念,而是同一笔账单的六个项目——你为其中一个付钱,账单会从另一个项目里扣。第六个(临界)不是并列项,而是前五个共同作用时出现的那道门槛。

结构与证据。5 部分 19 章、141 页、29 幅插图。四种读者分层(故事/多想一层/再想一层/给专家的注),每部分配生活故事与动手练习,另有"十二个常见误解""给老师、家长和孩子的一页""一天里的六个核心""一场三个人的谈话""二十五个真实的人""一年之后:一次诚实的复盘"。卷首《总纲　现实中不存在无限》把共同地基写在最前面:无限只作为极限,不作为现实系统的属性。

可复算的部分。书中每一个硬数字都指向单行本里的可复算单元:图拉普拉斯两阶段相变的临界耦合(Petersen 图 (0.200, 0.500)、环 C₁₀ (0.250, 2.618));完备/可组合/传递三角在 n=12、K=15 下的精确前沿(473721 种分配,max C=0.58、坐标和上界 ≈2.16);时间错配 τ 的三个情景(τ≈0.5/3.5/30)与两次事前登记的跨域盲测。附录 D 把这些单元与各自的"死穴"并排写出,读者可据此亲手检验或推翻。

诚实的学术纪律。每个核心都标出对应专著的证据等级(A／B／C),不把 C 级猜想当结论讲;凡通俗化损伤原意之处,以专著为准;本书不新增实证结论,也不解释一切——它只覆盖"有限的东西如何保持样子、又如何改变"这一条线。失败记录、可证伪登记簿与合并术语表都在各专著里,本书只做入口。

边界。它是入口,不是替代。三样是否真的至多取二,取决于预算是否紧(K、D 有界);τ 的取值依赖两个不可测的存量;六个核心对"人"这一层的解释力最弱。凡是书中说得像结论的地方,都对应一个在专著里被标为 B 或 C 的判断。

Abstract (English). This is the entry volume of the series (nine books, fourteen working papers): six core ideas turn six monographs into a book readable from age ten to specialist. It adds no new empirical claims; it only writes one and the same bill, and one and the same ruler, in a language that can be read, used and checked.

Core thesis. Behind the visible world lies an invisible bill with three prices: being precise, fitting together, carrying over. Under a tight budget at most two can be paid. The six cores (seeing, remembering, keeping up, choosing, telling, criticality) are not six parallel ideas but six items on one bill: paying for one deducts from another. The sixth is not a peer of the first five; it is the threshold that appears when the first five act together.

Structure and evidence. Five parts, nineteen chapters, 141 pages, 29 figures. Four reader levels (story / one layer deeper / one more / expert note); each part pairs everyday stories with hands-on exercises; further sections cover twelve common misconceptions, a page for teachers, parents and teenagers, the six cores in one day, a three-person conversation, twenty-five real people, and an honest review one year later. The opening chapter states the shared ground: infinity exists only as a limit, never as a property of a real system.

Recomputable parts. Every hard number points to a recomputable unit in a stand-alone volume: the critical couplings of the two-stage transition on graphs (Petersen graph (0.200, 0.500); cycle C₁₀ (0.250, 2.618)); the exact frontier of the completeness / composability / transitivity triangle at n=12, K=15 (473,721 allocations; max C=0.58, coordinate-sum bound ≈2.16); three regimes of temporal mismatch τ (≈0.5 / 3.5 / 30) and two pre-registered blind tests. Appendix D sets each unit beside its weak point so a reader can test or refute it.

Discipline. Each core carries the evidence grade of its source monograph (A/B/C); grade-C conjectures are never presented as conclusions; where popularisation distorts the original, the monograph prevails; the book adds no empirical claims and explains nothing outside "how a finite thing keeps its shape and how it changes". Failure logs, refutation registers and the merged glossary live in the monographs; this volume is the doorway.

Boundaries. It is an entry point, not a substitute. Whether the three properties really allow at most two depends on the budget being tight (K, D bounded); τ depends on two unmeasurable stocks; the six cores are weakest at the level of individual persons. Every sentence that reads like a conclusion corresponds to a B or C claim marked as such in a monograph.

Features. Fully open source: text, figures, scripts and build pipeline are reproducible (content CC BY 4.0 / code MIT); grading sits in the apparatus, not in every clause; the nine books and fourteen working papers are upstream and downstream of each other, and this volume doubles as the series' external registry (minimal verification units, cross-domain blind tests, merged glossary).
