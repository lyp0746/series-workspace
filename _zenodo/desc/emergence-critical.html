中文摘要。本著作提出"临界"的可操作定义与检验程序,用以回答一个古老但含混的问题:变化究竟在哪一刻,从"还是原来的东西"变成"另一个东西"?

核心主张。一次质变=系统在某个尺度上获得不可由微观无损推出的宏观序。它有三个可观测标记:序参量跃变、临界慢化、宏观描述比细节更好用;三个中两个成立,才叫质变。临界不是第六个并列概念,而是前五个核心共同作用时出现的那道门槛。

理论与应用。全书从"量变到质变为什么需要一门科学"开始,给出六件工具(序参量、对称破缺、临界慢化、标度与普适类、因果涌现、下向因果),再以五步流程与反例检验前五本书:区分、遗忘、时间、取舍、诠释。最后把视角推向形式化的地平线、不可通约、以及"不要把地平线当台阶"的警告。

实证与算例。本版包含四组可复算实验。二维 Ising 蒙特卡洛校准:T_c = 2.2645(精确 2.26919)、γ/ν = 1.752(精确 1.75);β/ν = 0.156 偏大 25%、ν 未测出,均如实报告。临界雪崩:m=1.0 时幂律指数 1.495(理论 3/2)。临界慢化:AR(1) 的恢复时间与方差在 5% 内吻合。有限尺度坍缩:同一批数据,少一个尺寸就可能翻转判定——这成为"诊断效力随尺度增长"的核心证据。

诚实的学术纪律。六个最小验证单元按"能不能真做"排序,并标明哪些已做、哪些只到部分通过;失败记录单独成节(附录 R);每张图都配脚本与随机种子;被合并章节中遗漏的推导(朗道、标度、KKT)恢复为附录 X,并注明是"补编"而非新内容。

预测与诊断的边界。多数社会案例只能到隐喻级;单一指标不能定向;慢化报"近",不报"何时"。框架的核心发现不是一条新常数,而是一条边界:诊断的效力与可观测尺度成正比——尺度不够时,两个竞争假设会一样好看。

学术特点。完全开源:实验脚本、数据、插图与构建流程可复现(CC BY 4.0 / MIT);方法论透明:分级、失败与"事后补算"均标注;工具:一页纸诊断表、六件工具速查、十个案例的对照打分。

Abstract (English). This work offers an operational definition and test procedure for criticality, answering an old but vague question: at which moment does a change turn from "the same thing" into "another thing"?

Core thesis. A qualitative transition is the emergence, at some scale, of macro-order that cannot be derived losslessly from the micro-level. It has three observable markers — order-parameter jump, critical slowing down, and macro-description being more useful than detail; two out of three are required. Criticality is not a sixth parallel concept but the threshold that appears when the first five cores act together.

Theory and applications. Six tools (order parameter, symmetry breaking, critical slowing down, scaling and universality, causal emergence, downward causation), a five-step procedure, and a test of the previous five books: distinction, forgetting, time, choice and interpretation. The book then moves to the horizon of formalisation, incommensurability, and the warning against mistaking a horizon for a step.

Experiments. Four reproducible experiments. 2D Ising Monte Carlo: T_c = 2.2645 (exact 2.26919), γ/ν = 1.752 (exact 1.75); β/ν = 0.156 (25% high) and ν unresolved, both reported. Critical avalanches: exponent 1.495 at m=1.0 (theory 3/2). Slowing down: AR(1) relaxation time and variance within 5%. Finite-size collapse: with one size fewer, the verdict can flip — the central evidence that diagnostic power scales with the observable scale.

Discipline. Six minimal verification units are ordered by feasibility and marked done, partial or open; failures have their own appendix; every figure ships with a script and a seed; derivations lost in an earlier merge (Landau, scaling, KKT) are restored as Appendix X and labelled as supplements.

Boundaries. Most social cases reach only metaphor; a single indicator cannot orient; slowing down reports "near", not "when". The central finding is a boundary, not a constant: diagnostic power scales with the observable scale.

Features. Fully open source (CC BY 4.0 / MIT); transparent method with grades, failures and post-hoc computations marked; tools: a one-page diagnostic sheet, a six-tool quick reference, and a ten-case scoring table.
