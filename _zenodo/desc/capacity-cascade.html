中文摘要。本书处理一个被反复提出、却极少被严格表述的问题：大语言模型的键值缓存、AI 智能体的上下文窗口与人类的记忆—学习系统，是否由同一机制主导？答案是一次有意的“降级”：三者不是同构的，而是同约束的——受同一组信息论约束（压缩、干扰、遗忘），却运行在相差九个数量级的时间尺度上，形成从毫秒到终身的“容量级联”。遗忘因此不是记忆的失败，而是学习的边界条件。

核心主张。有限容量下的记忆、智能与自我。本书不写概论，只把一个问题的所有侧面做深：一个有限的东西，怎样一边保持结构，一边还能变化。本版的改动集中在一处——把“离线计算”拆成两个方向。

结构与证据。7 部分、25 章、324 页、附录 A–F、28 幅图。新增第 12 章《对账：离线计算为什么需要真值》，并把第四部分重写为“离线、窗口与调度”：睡眠（第 10 章）、梦作为内源重组（第 11 章）、对账作为有真值的离线计算（第 12 章）、潜意识（第 13 章）、元认知（第 14 章）。

实证与算例。第 12 章给出一次可复算的替身实验（40 个事实、60 个会话、20 个种子、四个臂）。三条结果：只合并不核对等于什么都没做（0.639 对 0.643，存储降到五分之一）；对账的收益近似正比于可验证性（Δa≈0.159ρ，只有理论上界的四成）；整合周期越长正确率越低（k 由 1 到 32，0.933→0.656），而“有把握的错”由 0 升到 0.299。四条预注册否证条件中三条未触发、一条触发——触发的那条（合并不制造自信的错误）按原样保留，不事后改写。脚本 analysis/exp_dream.py，协议 analysis/PREREG_exp_dream.md。

诚实的学术纪律。结论强度不超过证据强度。厂商公开的效果数字一律只取方向、不取幅度：客户自报的“约 6 倍”不进入本书任何一处。对“做梦”功能的装置事实记为 A 级（功能、触发条件、审核开关可查），效果记为 C 级（单次运行、无方差、无独立复现、不公开负例）。附录 C.6 追加 P13—P15 并当场裁决，明确写出“本节打破 C.4 的一句话”，因为机制层的部分命中不能升格为现象层的证据。

边界。本书给坐标与判据，不给判决；不替代领域专家材料，也不把类比写成定理。第 12 章与第 22 章只用工程材料的“结构”，不用其“幅度”；第 11 章的“梦=正则化”仍是最低等级假说，并且现在有了一个明确的对照物：2026 年到场的是对账那一半，不是内源重组那一半。

学术特点。把三个载体（模型、智能体、人）放在同一张桌上，用同一组量比较，并用证据分级而非修辞来分配置信度。本版新增的判别式是“读不读真值”：它把原本只能按证据强度排序的 H1/H2，变成了可以被关掉一根线来检验的分界，而且这个检验不需要先回答“机器有没有梦”。

Abstract.The Boundary of Forgetting: Memory, Intelligence and the Self under Finite Capacity. The KV cache of a language model, the context window of an agent and human memory are not isomorphic but iso-constrained: bound by one set of information-theoretic limits across roughly nine orders of magnitude in timescale. Forgetting is therefore the condition of learning, not its failure.

Core thesis.How does something finite keep its structure while still being able to change? This edition makes one structural change: offline computation is split into two directions.

Structure and evidence.7 parts, 26 chapters, 324 pages, appendices A–F, 28 figures. A new chapter 12, “Reconciliation: Why Offline Computation Needs Ground Truth”, and a rebuilt Part IV — sleep (ch. 10), dreaming as endogenous reorganisation (ch. 11), reconciliation as offline computation with ground truth (ch. 12), the subconscious (ch. 13), metacognition (ch. 14).

Evidence and computation.Chapter 12 reports a reproducible stand-in experiment (40 facts, 60 sessions, 20 seeds, four arms). Merging without checking buys nothing (0.639 vs 0.643, storage down to a fifth); the gain from reconciliation scales with verifiability (Δa≈0.159ρ, about four tenths of the theoretical bound); longer consolidation cycles monotonically hurt (k from 1 to 32: 0.933→0.656) while confident-but-wrong answers rise from 0.000 to 0.299. Three of four preregistered falsification conditions were not triggered; the one that was is reported as-is.

Discipline.Conclusion strength never exceeds evidence strength. Vendor-reported effects are used for direction only, never for magnitude; the client-reported “about 6×” appears nowhere in the book. The existence of a scheduled consolidation stage is graded A (the function, its triggers and its review switch are inspectable); its effects are graded C (single runs, no variance, no independent replication, no published negative cases). Appendix C.6 adds P13–P15 and adjudicates them on the spot, stating explicitly that it breaks the appendix's own claim that no entry had observations.

Boundaries.The book gives coordinates and criteria, not verdicts. It does not dress an analogy as a theorem. What arrived in 2026 is the reconciliation half of offline computation, not the endogenous-reorganisation half; the book says so in the text rather than in a footnote.

Features.Three carriers — model, agent, human — compared with the same quantities, with confidence assigned by an evidence scale rather than by rhetoric. The new discriminator is “does it read ground truth”: it turns a ranking by evidence strength into a boundary that can be tested by switching one wire off, without first answering whether machines dream.
