中文摘要。本书处理一个被反复提出、却极少被严格表述的问题：大语言模型的键值缓存、AI 智能体的上下文窗口与人类的记忆—学习系统，是否由同一机制主导？答案是一次有意的“降级”：三者不是同构的，而是同约束的——受同一组信息论约束（压缩、干扰、遗忘），却运行在相差九个数量级的时间尺度上，形成从毫秒到终身的“容量级联”。遗忘因此不是记忆的失败，而是学习的边界条件。

背景与问题。本件是《有限系统的取舍》系列第 2 件，接在《错位的时间》之后，处理「遗忘的边界」这一支：把母题落到一类可核对的材料上。

方法与结构。7 部分、25 章、324 页、附录 A–F、28 幅图。新增第 12 章《对账：离线计算为什么需要真值》，并把第四部分重写为“离线、窗口与调度”：睡眠（第 10 章）、梦作为内源重组（第 11 章）、对账作为有真值的离线计算（第 12 章）、潜意识（第 13 章）、元认知（第 14 章）。 全书 7 部分、25 章，附录 A–F。

主要结果。①容量不是数，而是随干扰衰减的曲线，半衰点可测；②多时间尺度级联是有限容量下的必要结构，单一时间常数无法同时获得适应与稳定；③大模型的位置偏差是随位置累积的更新误差，上下文漂移是目标在长上下文里被反复重估；④离线计算须拆成内源重组与对账两条路，只有后者能变正确（0.639 对 0.643，存储降到 1/5）；⑤三件套（书记员、异构重建、带锚裁决）在真实系统中仍不齐备。

Abstract.The Boundary of Forgetting: Memory, Intelligence and the Self under Finite Capacity. The KV cache of a language model, the context window of an agent and human memory are not isomorphic but iso-constrained: bound by one set of information-theoretic limits across roughly nine orders of magnitude in timescale. Forgetting is therefore the condition of learning, not its failure.

Background.This is item 2 of the series, following Desynchronized Time; it tests the series motif on the material named in the title.

Method and structure.7 parts, 25 chapters, appendices A–F.

Results.(i) Capacity is a curve that decays with interference, not a number; its half-point is measurable. (ii) A multi-timescale cascade is necessary under finite capacity: no single time constant can buy both adaptation and stability. (iii) Positional bias in LLMs is an update error accumulating with position; context drift is the goal being re-priced over long contexts. (iv) Offline computation splits into endogenous recombination and reconciliation; only the latter can become correct (0.639 vs 0.643, storage at one fifth). (v) The trio—clerk, heterogeneous reconstruction, anchored adjudication—is still nowhere complete in deployed systems.

Contributions.It contributes three things: it states the problem as checkable conditions; gives reproducible readings or structures; and publishes its boundaries, failures and retractions.
