中文摘要。一个流行的说法是：有志气的人正在用 agent 扩张能力，这像启蒙思想家梦寐以求的「开明君主制」——人以理性和经验定方向，agent 负责实现；而当足够多的人这么做时，就会重演轴心时代的百家争鸣。这个说法有一个好直觉（执行权在扩散），也有一个未受检验的推论（红利会随之扩散）。本文把它拆成三个价目表能回答的问题：一次「新判断」比一次「重放」贵多少？执行单价真的在降吗？钱最后停在谁那一层？数据是四份公开快照：438 个模型的价目与托管读数、九个实验室的一手价目页与席位页、平台方每周发布的用量口径。

主要读数。（1）重放—判断价差（RJS＝输出单价 ÷ 缓存读单价）在 294 个模型上的中位 34.5 倍；闭源 50、开放 19；自家价目页上 12—200 倍（DeepSeek 与 Claude Fable 5.1 均为 200），而多托管市场把同一批模型摊平到 19 倍——重放被竞争压到底，判断由厂商自己写。（2）「执行变便宜」是同名不同物：按同能力算降约 150 倍（单一来源）；按「当月进入市场的新模型标价中位」算，闭源 $6.00→$5.00、开放 $0.65→$0.60（不降）；且 Google 官方公告 Gemini 4 Argon 自 2026-12-01 起输出价 $10→$20。（3）量与钱分离：周 token 量 0.5 万亿→128.4 万亿（约 250 倍／20 个月），开放权重拿 62—79% 的 token，闭源仍拿 73% 的消费额。（4）双层是常态：9 家里 6 家同时经营「权重可下载、几十家可托管」的商品层与「只能在自己平台上用」的旗舰层，溢价 2.9—83 倍（中位 21.3）。（5）席位不随执行降：8 个公开价位 $12.6—$200；ChatGPT Plus 三年仍是 $20。（6）低价的一半是数据：首方最低价以训练权为对价，不训练的第三方托管约 2 倍价。

边界（含三处自我打击）。其一，原假设「溢价来自不可传递性」被自己的数据否证：T 分与溢价无单调关系（T=4 的 OpenAI 83.3 倍，T=3 的月之暗面 2.9 倍），T8 登记为否证。其二，RJS 与上下文长度 ρ=0.53，按上下文四档分组后「闭源＞开放」只在两档成立，T2 降为部分。其三，「假竞争」这个词站不住——竞争是真的、只是分两层，改判为两层竞争。另有两条一般边界：数据只覆盖「通过第三方 API 使用模型」的市场，不含企业私有部署与消费端使用；全部价目是标价，不是实付价。

Abstract (English). A popular claim holds that ambitious people are using agents to extend their own capacity, reviving the enlightened despotism that Enlightenment thinkers wished for, and that the Axial Age will repeat itself. The intuition (execution is being democratised) is sound; the inference (so are the returns) has not been tested. This paper asks three questions a price sheet can answer: how much more does a fresh judgement cost than a replay; is the unit price of execution actually falling; and on which tier does the money stop? Four public snapshots supply the data. Six results. (1) The replay–judgement spread (RJS = output price / cache-read price) has a median of 34.5x across 294 models (closed 50x, open 19x), reaching 12–200x on first-party sheets while the multi-host market compresses the same models to 19x: replay is priced by competition, judgement by the vendor. (2) "Execution is getting cheaper" names two objects: capability-adjusted prices fell ~150x (single source), while the median list price of models entering the market fell only from $6.00 to $5.00 (closed) and $0.65 to $0.60 (open); Google schedules Gemini 4 Argon from $10 to $20 on 2026-12-01. (3) Volume and money separate: weekly tokens rose from 0.5T to 128.4T, open weights hold 62–79% of tokens, and closed weights still take 73% of spend. (4) Two tiers are the norm: six of nine labs sell both a self-hostable and a first-party-only tier, with premiums of 2.9–83x (median 21.3x). (5) Seat prices do not follow execution prices down ($12.6–$200; ChatGPT Plus has been $20 for three years). (6) Half of a low price is data: first-party minimum prices are paid for with training rights, while no-train hosts charge about twice as much. Three self-inflicted wounds are reported: the transferability explanation of the premium is falsified by the paper's own data; RJS turns out to be largely a function of context length (rho = 0.53), demoting T2 to partial; and "counterfeit competition" is renamed "two-tier competition".
