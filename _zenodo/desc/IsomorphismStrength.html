贡献。本件的贡献有三：把「同构的强度」写成可检验的条件；给出可复算的读数或结构；公开边界、失败与撤回。

边界。四级阶梯是判据，不是度量：它判「能不能写成复合律」，不能判「这个类比值多少」。四十条是一个封闭集，因此附录 P 的第四十一条只登记、不并入统计。本文最大的自我限制写在正文第 11 章：把本文自己的四十条声明也过一遍探针——能通过的那几条，都不是本文最想说的那几条。

Abstract.This paper prices the claim that two fields resemble each other. It proposes a four-rung ladder — strict, adjunction, functor, metaphor — and requires every cross-domain declaration to supply an observable fracture probe: the point at which the resemblance breaks. Forty declarations are coded across five motif groups (duality, universal property, invariant, structure, process). Distribution: strict 12, adjunction 11, functor 12, metaphor 5 — strict isomorphism covers under a third. Fracture layer: composition 16, equivalence 12, morphism 8, object 4 — seventy per cent of fractures are not at the object layer, where almost all public argument sits. Negative transfer: 15 of 40 (38%), and it does not fall monotonically with strength (strict 17%, adjunction 60%, functor 50%, metaphor 36%): the dangerous rungs are the ones that look like proofs. This edition adds Appendix P, a forty-first declaration ("the person is the sovereign, the agent the subject"), coded at the metaphor rung with its fracture at the composition layer; four of its five probes cut immediately, because a subject that cannot rebel is a tool, not a subject. It is kept outside the closed set of forty, so every proportion in the main text is unchanged.
