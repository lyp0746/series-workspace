中文摘要。本合集把同一系列的九本书与十四篇工作论文装订成一册,另附卷首一篇非学术文本(《中道周行集》,诗词散骈赋铭六体,为作者的方法自述,不参与分级)。合集只做装订:每件保留自己的题名、作者行、版权页、图表、附录与登记簿,正文一字未改;合集另加总封面、书名页、版权与说明、卷首、总序、编者说明、合集一览、六核心速查、总目录、各部分过渡语、后记与版本记。

核心主张。二十三件作品放在同一张桌子上,只为了看清一件事:一个有限的东西,怎样一边保持自己的样子,一边还能改变?六句话在这一册里第一次可以互相看见——先划线、线划下之后要丢东西、丢完之后还得赶上时间、赶时间就要让出一样、连讲述也是分配、让出的东西积累到某个点就会变天。

结构与材料。第一部 九卷专著:只能选两样(导览)、区分的语法、遗忘的边界、错位的时间、选择的代价、诠释的权衡、涌现的临界、心灵的分岔、热点的账单。第二部 实证论文六篇:术语的账单、谁在切时间轴、线外的残余、明清湘中驿道、轮换的让渡、被点燃的生态。第三部 方法论文与阶段总账:优化的边界、容量的三个代理、领域的剖面、同构的强度、记忆的锚点、锚点的下限、开明君主与两层竞争;末为《六张账单的对账》,覆盖一—十四、共 110 条登记命题。

证据与对账。合集不替任何一件说话:每一件的证据等级、失败记录与可证伪条件都留在它自己的附录里。可读的横向证据只有一类——对账:110 条登记命题中 16 条被自身数据打掉(14.5%);七处"同名不同物"被逐条列出;个体层 0 条、Ψ 未测、容量三代理三方向,至今仍是开放项而不是欠债。

诚实的学术纪律。合集不改写任何一件的正文,因此也不替任何一件背书;凡合集里读起来比单行本更顺的地方,一律以单行本为准。版次、页数与各件的版本一一对应,不合并、不重排、不统一编号。

边界。它是阅读便利,不是权威版本:引用请以各件单行本为准(含其 DOI、附录与可证伪登记簿)。合集的章节号、图表号与证据分级与单行本一致,故不能当作"统一重排后的新版本"引用;卷首的诗词文本不进任何统计。

Abstract (English). This collection binds the series' nine books and fourteen working papers into one volume, preceded by a non-academic frontispiece (Zhongdao Zhouxing Ji — poems, lyrics, prose, parallel prose, rhapsody and inscriptions stating the author's method in another language, taking no part in grading). The collection only binds: every item keeps its own title page, colophon, figures, appendices and register, with the body text unchanged. It adds a general cover, title page, copyright note, frontispiece, general preface, editor's note, collection overview, six-core quick reference, general table of contents, part transitions, afterword and version note.

Core thesis. Twenty-three works on one table, to see one thing clearly: how does a finite thing keep its shape while still changing? Six sentences become visible to each other for the first time in this volume — draw the line first; after the line, something must be dropped; after dropping, you still have to keep up; keeping up costs one of the three; even telling is allocation; and what has been given up accumulates until the weather changes.

Structure and materials. Part I, nine monographs: Pick Two (a guide), The Grammar of Distinction, The Boundary of Forgetting, Desynchronized Time, The Price of Choice, The Interpretation Tradeoff, The Criticality of Emergence, The Forking Mind, The Bill of the Hot List. Part II, six empirical papers: terminology, periodisation, delayed recognition, relay roads, official rotation, ignited ecosystems. Part III, method papers and the stage ledger: optimisation, capacity proxies, domain cross-section, isomorphism strength, memory anchoring, anchoring floor, sovereign competition; closing with Reconciling Six Ledgers, which covers papers I–XIV and 110 registered propositions.

Evidence and reconciliation. The collection speaks for no single item: every evidence grade, failure log and refutation condition stays in that item's own appendix. The only cross-cutting evidence is reconciliation: of 110 registered propositions, 16 were killed by their own data (14.5%); seven cases of "same name, different object" are listed one by one; the individual level (0 propositions), Ψ (unmeasured) and the three capacity proxies (three directions) remain open, not owed.

Discipline. The collection rewrites no item's body text, and therefore endorses none; wherever the collection reads more smoothly than a stand-alone volume, the stand-alone volume prevails. Edition data and page counts correspond to each item's own version; nothing is merged, re-ordered or renumbered.

Boundaries. It is a reading convenience, not the authoritative version: cite the stand-alone editions (with their DOIs, appendices and refutation registers). Chapter, figure and evidence-grade numbering follow the stand-alone editions, so it must not be cited as a re-edited new edition; the frontispiece takes part in no statistic.

Features. Fully open source (content CC BY 4.0 / code MIT); one artefact, one DOI, one version per DOI, and the collection carries its own concept DOI (10.5281/zenodo.23613406); all twenty-four items on Zenodo now carry a CC BY 4.0 licence and keywords, so they can be found, cited and split apart again.
