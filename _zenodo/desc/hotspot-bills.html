中文摘要。本著作是系列的方法演示:用六个核心,把过去一年被反复讨论的公共事件做成一张张"诊断卡"。它不判对错,只诊断这场争论卡在哪一层、让出了什么、谁在付账。

核心主张。热搜为什么总让人越看越看不懂?因为讨论被压缩、被分层、被时间错配,而读者往往在错误的楼层里找答案。六把尺子——看见(在划什么线、谁被排除)、记住(被压成什么标签、丢了什么)、跟上(快钟与慢钟差多少)、选择(各方要哪两样、让出哪一样)、讲述(争事实还是抢位置)、临界(还是原来那个东西吗)——不问"谁对",只问"卡在哪"。

数据与方法。案例全部来自真实公开存档:知乎热榜每日存档,2025-10-16 至 2026-10-16,338 天、2875 个不同话题;X 趋势按国家按日期的历史存档,美/英/日,81 个"国家—日"采样。全书 18 章、100 页,含 71 张诊断卡(65 张标准卡、5 张长卡、30 条一句话诊断),另有季度复盘、方法自检、十个反例、一个 90 分钟工作坊与数据附录。

实证与算例。季度复盘用脚本统计四季度天数、话题日总数、前五占比,并如实标注 Q4 缺日、不可比总量;数据附录列出各季前 30 名;每张卡都带证据等级与可反驳条件;十个反例逐条说明"什么情况下这张卡是错的"。

诚实的学术纪律。诊断的是讨论的结构,不是事件的对错;数据缺口与口径问题逐条写明;平台数据的局限(平台≠公众、热度≠重要性、天数≠因果)写在正文;"上榜天数"只作代理变量,并给出其失效条件。

预测与诊断的边界。案例会过期,方法不会;它不预测、不判决,也不替代一手来源。它提供的是一个可以重算的诊断流程:选事件、填六格、写可反驳条件、三个月后对账。

学术特点。完全开源:采集与统计脚本、原始数据、插图与构建流程可复现(CC BY 4.0 / MIT);方法论透明:每个数字都能由脚本重新得到;工具:诊断卡模板、六核速查、一页纸诊断表、90 分钟工作坊流程。

Abstract (English). This is the series' methodological demonstration: six cores turn a year of contested public events into diagnostic cards. It does not judge right or wrong; it diagnoses on which layer the argument is stuck, what it gives up, and who pays.

Core thesis. Why does the hot list leave us more confused the longer we look? Because discussion is compressed, layered and temporally misaligned, while readers look for answers on the wrong floor. Six lenses — seeing (what line is drawn, who is excluded), remembering (what label survives, what is lost), keeping up (how far apart the fast and slow clocks are), choosing (which two each side wants, which one it gives up), telling (facts or positions), criticality (is it still the same thing) — ask not "who is right" but "where is it stuck".

Data and method. All cases come from real public archives: daily Zhihu hot-list archives, 2025-10-16 to 2026-10-16, 338 days and 2,875 distinct topics; X trend archives by country and date for the US, UK and Japan, 81 country–day samples. 18 chapters, 100 pages, 71 diagnostic cards (65 standard, 5 long-form, 30 one-line), plus a quarterly review, a method self-audit, ten counterexamples, a 90-minute workshop and data appendices.

Evidence. The quarterly review computes days, topic-days and top-five shares by script, and states plainly that Q4 has missing days and cannot be compared on totals; appendices list the top 30 per quarter; every card carries an evidence grade and a refutation condition; ten counterexamples state when each card is wrong.

Discipline. The object of diagnosis is the structure of the argument, not the facts of the case; data gaps and measurement caveats are stated; platform limitations (platform ≠ public, heat ≠ importance, days ≠ causation) are in the main text; "days on the list" is only a proxy, with its failure conditions given.

Boundaries. Cases expire, the method does not; it neither predicts nor judges, and never replaces primary sources. It offers a recomputable procedure: pick an event, fill six cells, write a refutation condition, check back in three months.

Features. Fully open source (CC BY 4.0 / MIT): collection and statistics scripts, raw data, figures and build pipeline; transparent methodology: every number can be recomputed; tools: a card template, a six-lens quick reference, a one-page diagnostic sheet, and a 90-minute workshop.
