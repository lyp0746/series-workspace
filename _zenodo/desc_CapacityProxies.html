中文摘要。《遗忘的边界》的容量—压缩—遗忘有一个始终没补上的缺口：**容量从未被测量**。《线外的残余》补上了"恢复判据"，但它的容量改写为"合法问题名额"，不可计数；《六张账单的对账》进一步指出，六篇 48 条登记命题里没有一条以容量为对象。本文要做的是一件很小的事：**给容量找三个可数的代理，用既有数据检验一句直觉——"容量越大，忘得越少"**。三个代理是：**分期槽位**（一部世界史教材使用多少个顶层边界）、**编码槽位**（一个县的驿铺条目数与并镇次数）、**标签槽位**（一条 topic 线的日均新建量与合格率）。数据全部来自三篇既有论文的原始编码，不新增采集。结果是三个不同的答案。第一，**分期槽位支持直觉**：槽位越多，越少依赖"通货门"（1500、1914）；分箱中位从 0.50 降到 0.33、0.20、0.14，Pearson r=−0.245（51 部来源）。第二，**编码槽位与直觉相反**：驿铺条目越多，地名保留率越低（r=−0.682，n=6），但它与"撤县年数"高度混淆（r=−0.835），因此判"不可判"而不是"反向成立"。第三，**标签槽位也与直觉相反**：日均新建越大，合格率反而越高（r=0.932，n=4），这条直接证伪了"名额紧张导致质量稀释"。三个代理合起来给出一句冷结论：**容量不是保护，至少不是单方向的保护**；槽位越多，压缩时能失去的也越多。本文同时给出三个代理的定义、算式与局限，并把七条命题登记入册（一条被自己证伪、两条开放）。它不填满"容量"那一格，但它把那一格从"无测量"推进到"有三种可算的痕迹，其中两种不按直觉走"。 **关键词**：容量；代理；槽位；压缩；遗忘；保留率；合格率；证据分级 ---

Abstract (English). The capacity–compression–forgetting framework has one hole that has never been closed: capacity itself has never been measured. *Residuals Outside the Line* supplied a criterion for recovery but rewrote capacity as "slots for legitimate questions", which cannot be counted; *Reconciling Six Ledgers* showed that none of the 48 registered propositions takes capacity as its object. This paper does one small thing: it proposes three countable proxies for capacity and tests one intuition — "more capacity, less forgetting" — on existing data. The proxies are **periodization slots** (how many top-level boundaries a world-history source uses), **coding slots** (a county's relay-station entries and consolidations), and **label slots** (daily new repositories against the pass rate of a topic line). Results diverge. First, periodization slots **support** the intuition: the more slots, the less a source relies on "currency gates" (1500, 1914), with binned medians falling 0.50 → 0.33 → 0.20 → 0.14 (Pearson r=−0.245, 51 sources). Second, coding slots run **against** it: more relay entries, lower toponym retention (r=−0.682, n=6), but confounded with years since county-to-city conversion (r=−0.835), so the verdict is "undecidable" rather than "reversed". Third, label slots also run against it: larger inflow, higher pass rate (r=0.932, n=4), which directly falsifies the "crowded slots dilute quality" story. The joint conclusion is cold: **capacity is not protection, or at least not unidirectional protection**; more slots also means more to lose when compression comes. **Keywords**: capacity; proxy; slot; compression; forgetting; retention; pass rate; evidence grading ---
