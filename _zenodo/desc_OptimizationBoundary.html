中文摘要。"优化"是当代最容易被滥用的动词之一。它听起来像方法，实际上是一个**四前提结构**：要有目标函数、要有搜索空间、要有预算与时钟、要有停止规则。缺任何一项，优化就不再是优化，而是另一种东西——指标失真、锁定、无限投入或反噬。本文把"优化"从六核心之外拉回来，安放在它们之间：**目标函数由"讲述"提供**（给竞争指标分配置信度），**搜索空间由"看见"划定**（划一条线，决定哪些解在域内），**预算与时钟由"记住"与"跟上"提供**（容量与窗口），**停止规则由"临界"判定**（何时该停、规则何时换）。据此，优化不是第七个核心，而是一个**横切四核心的"搜索—评估—停止"循环**；把它升为核心，就会把"取舍"误读成"调参"。本文编码 20 个越界案例（目标 7、空间 5、停止 5、预算 2、反例 1），逐条记录被违反的前提、越界机制、后果与强度，得到三个结果。第一，**目标函数越界最普遍（7/20），且后果多为"指标失真"**：苏联的总产值指标、教育考试的分数、学术评价的影响因子、VaR 的风险约束，都是"以可计算者为目标"的同一形状。第二，**停止规则越界的后果最重（5 例中 4 例为"重"）**：强化降糖（ACCORD）、抗生素常规预防、农药加量、算法最大化停留——它们的共同点是"没有退出条件"。第三，**预算越界最容易被忽略（仅 2 例被记录）**：因为新建可计入政绩、维护不能。反例章用"进化不是优化"作结：自然选择没有目标函数、没有全局停止规则，只有局部搜索与压缩账单；这也是本文拒绝把"优化"提升为普遍框架的理由。全文七条命题（T1—T7）中两条支持、一条部分、两条待检验、一条开放、一条被自己撤回，按系列纪律登记。 **关键词**：优化；目标函数；搜索空间；停止规则；局部最优；指标失真；六核心；证据分级 ---

Abstract (English). "Optimization" is among the most abused verbs of the age. It sounds like a method; it is in fact a **four-premise structure**: a target function, a search space, a budget and clock, and a stopping rule. Remove any one, and optimization becomes something else — metric distortion, lock-in, unbounded investment, or backlash. This paper pulls optimization back from outside the six cores and places it between them: the target function is supplied by *interpretation* (allocating confidence across competing metrics), the search space by *distinction* (drawing the line that defines the feasible set), the budget and clock by *memory* and *timing*, and the stopping rule by *criticality*. Optimization is therefore not a seventh core but a **search–evaluate–stop loop cutting across four cores**; raising it to a core mistakes trade-offs for tuning. Twenty cases of overreach are coded (target 7, space 5, stopping 5, budget 2, one counter-example), each with the violated premise, mechanism, outcome and severity. Three results. First, target overreach is the most common (7/20) and mostly yields metric distortion — Soviet gross-output targets, exam scores, journal impact factors, VaR constraints share one shape: taking the computable as the goal. Second, stopping-rule overreach has the heaviest consequences (4 of 5 "severe"): intensive glucose control, routine antibiotic prophylaxis, pesticide escalation, engagement maximization all lack an exit condition. Third, budget overreach is the least recorded (2 cases) because new construction counts and maintenance does not. A counter-example chapter closes with "evolution is not optimization": natural selection has no target function and no global stopping rule, only local search and a compression bill. **Keywords**: optimization; objective function; search space; stopping rule; local optimum; metric distortion; six cores; evidence grading ---
