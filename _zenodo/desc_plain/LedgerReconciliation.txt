本文做什么。
第一，给出 126 条命题的公开编码，任何人可以用 
ledger.csv

方法与数据。
数据来自各篇登记簿附录，逐行抽取，不筛选；状态归一为七值（成立／支持／部分／待检验／开放／证伪／撤回），主核与尺度各归一档。数据只有一份

；8 图、多张表与交互页都由同一份数据生成。对账不合并效应量，不评哪一篇更好，不新增核心，不复述各篇结论，不把「对账」写成「统一」。

边界与失败条件。
对账不产生新证据，只改变可判断性；126 条是「作者愿意写成可证伪句子的那些」，不代表各篇的全部主张；状态的归一有判断成分，尤其「部分」与「支持」的界线；十三处冲突的消解规则是本文提出的，不是各篇作者的共识。本文也把自己放进表：六条命题两条待检验、两条支持、两条成立；T4 与 T5 是「成立」的，但它们是关于本系列的计数，不是关于世界的断言。

与系列的关系。
本篇是系列论文的方法学总账，与六本学术专著是「借用语言」关系，与十五篇工作论文是「二次编码」关系。第十五篇《接缝不在奇点》把物理侧的 30 条账目带进总账；本篇把它们压缩成 10 条登记命题，并把六组物理侧同名不同物并入第 5 章。后续每加一篇，都应把命题登记进本表，使对账持续。

Abstract.
 This paper reconciles rather than reviews. The 
120 propositions
 in the registers of fourteen of the fifteen working papers, plus the 
6
 propositions of this paper itself and the 
10
 of 
The Seam Is Not at the Singularity
, are extracted and re-coded on eight columns (paper, id, claim, test, result, status, grade, core, scale), then audited three ways. This edition adds this paper's own six propositions to the master table and incorporates six physical cases of “same name, different object”; old rows are unchanged.

What the paper does.
 It publishes the coding so that every count can be recomputed from 
ledger.csv
; it lists 
thirteen
 cases of “same name, different object” — the original seven (τ, capacity, gate, memory, compression, cheap, transferability) plus six from physics (locality, vacuum, information, symmetry, background, entropy) — with a carrier-layering rule; it gives three cross-paper tables (scale, clock, failure modes) and a five-layer dependency figure; and it names three structural blanks: no individual-scale proposition, only one lineage-scale counter-example, and a long-unmeasured capacity end.

Main readings.
 Status: 
86 closed (68.3%), 32 unclosed (25.4%), 8 withdrawn (6.3%)
; 10 falsified plus 8 withdrawn, 
18 (14.3%)
 killed by the authors' own data. Grade: 
A 3 / B 63 / C 60
 — the cores the series most wants to claim (criticality, choice, capacity) carry the lowest grades. Scale: proposition 92, community 19, institution 14, 
individual 0, lineage 1 (a counter-example)
. Cores: distinction 29, interpretation 28, memory 27, choice 23, criticality 11, timing 8; memory is the only large core with zero “established”, timing the only one with more withdrawals than establishments. The fifteenth paper adds ten physics-side propositions: 4 logical contradictions, 13 missing interfaces, 8 accounting conflicts, 5 theorem boundaries; none of the logical contradictions has been directly measured.

Method and data.
 Data come from the registers of the individual papers, extracted row by row without filtering. Status is normalised to seven values; core and scale are normalised to one category each. There is one data source, 
data/ledger.py
, exported to 
ledger.csv
; the 8 figures, the tables and the interactive page are generated from it. The paper does not pool effect sizes, rank papers, add a core, restate conclusions, or turn reconciliation into unification.

Boundaries and failure conditions.
 Reconciliation produces no new evidence; it changes judgement, not knowledge. The 126 propositions are those the authors were willing to write as falsifiable sentences, not all claims in the papers. Status normalisation involves judgement, especially the line between “partial” and “support”. The carrier-layering rule is this paper's proposal, not a consensus of the individual authors. The paper also puts itself in the table: of its six propositions, two are pending, two supported, two established; T4 and T5 are “established” only as counts about the series, not as claims about the world.

Relation to the series.
 This is the methodological master ledger of the series: it borrows the language of the six academic monographs and re-codes the fifteen working papers. 
The Seam Is Not at the Singularity
 brings 30 physics-side ledger items into the account; this paper compresses them into ten registered propositions and adds six physical cases of “same name, different object” to Chapter 5. Every new paper should register its propositions here.
