Abstract.Some propositions are neither refuted nor used. Coding twenty-four cases of delayed recognition (eighteen recoveries, three re-inventions, three permanent silences), the paper shows that the median silence is 37.5 years while the median lag from decoder availability to recognition is zero (never above five); that the old-paradigm coordinate predicts the return path; that re-invention occurs only where the proposition is re-derivable from the new framework's own resources (3/3 versus 2/18, Fisher p=0.0075); and that the three markers cannot be used as a score, since two permanently silent theories also hit two of three — what separates them is a necessary condition, the decoder (18/18 versus 0/6).
