中文摘要。有些命题提出时无人反对也无人理睬，几十年后才被“重新发现”：孟德尔沉寂 34 年，魏格纳 48 年，玻尔兹曼 36 年。本文把这种“延迟承认”当作记忆问题处理：命题被埋没不是因为共同体忘了它，而是因为旧范式把它压进了线外；它能回来也不是因为有人重读旧文献，而是因为另一套工具使旧残余重新可读。

背景与问题。本件是《有限系统的取舍》系列第 12 件，接在《谁在切时间轴》之后，处理「线外的残余」这一支：把母题落到一类可核对的材料上。

主要结果。①十八例恢复的沉寂期中位 37.5 年（15—2156），解码器出现到被承认的中位滞后 0 年（18/18 ≤5）；②三例重发明全部发生在可被新框架重建之时（3/3 对 2/18，Fisher p=0.0075）；③决定能否恢复的不是命中几条，而是有没有解码器（恢复 18/18，非恢复 0/6）；④恢复是分层的：方法 17/18、作者 13/18、术语 10/18；⑤两条结构性沉默路径：载体销毁与残余被吸收。

Abstract.Some propositions are neither refuted nor used. Coding twenty-four cases of delayed recognition (eighteen recoveries, three re-inventions, three permanent silences), the paper shows that the median silence is 37.5 years while the median lag from decoder availability to recognition is zero (never above five); that the old-paradigm coordinate predicts the return path; that re-invention occurs only where the proposition is re-derivable from the new framework's own resources (3/3 versus 2/18, Fisher p=0.0075); and that the three markers cannot be used as a score, since two permanently silent theories also hit two of three — what separates them is a necessary condition, the decoder (18/18 versus 0/6). Recovery is layered: method 17/18, authorship 13/18, terminology 10/18.

Background.This is item 12 of the series, following 谁在切时间轴; it tests the series motif on the material named in the title.

Method and structure.12 sections, at most six appendices.

Results.(i) For 18 recovered cases the median dormancy is 37.5 years (15–2156); the median lag from decoder availability to recognition is zero (18/18 within five years). (ii) All three reinventions occurred when a new framework could rebuild the claim (3/3 vs 2/18, Fisher p=0.0075). (iii) What decides recovery is not the number of markers hit but the presence of a decoder (18/18 vs 0/6). (iv) Recovery is layered: method 17/18, author 13/18, terminology 10/18. (v) Two structural paths to silence: carrier destruction and residue absorption.
