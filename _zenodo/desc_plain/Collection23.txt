核心主张：看得见的世界背后都有一张看不见的账单，账单上只有三个价格——看得准、拼得上、传得下去，最多付得起两个；让出的那一个，就是每一卷要指认的东西。六个核心（看见、记住、跟上、选择、讲述、临界）不是六门学问，而是同一笔账的六个项目；它们不是并列的六个词，而是「起点—约束—枢纽—元层—门槛」的五层结构。

Abstract (English). This volume binds twenty-three items of one series — nine books (six monographs, two popular readers, one popular-science monograph), fourteen working papers, and a frontispiece in classical Chinese — to ask a single question: how does something finite keep its shape while still being able to change? The core claim is a bill with three prices — being precise, fitting together, carrying over — of which at most two can be paid. This sixth edition adds the fourteenth paper (the replay–judgement spread has a median of 34.5×; six of nine labs run a self-hostable and a first-party-only tier with premiums of 2.9–83×; open weights hold 62–79% of tokens while closed weights take 73% of spend), the frontispiece Zhongdao Zhouxing Ji, a second edition of the tenth paper (a single-case falsification), a second edition of the eleventh (the forty-first declaration, at the metaphor rung), and a third edition of the ledger (62 → 110 propositions across fourteen papers).
