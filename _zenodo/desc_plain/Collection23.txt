中文摘要。
本著作是同一系列二十四件作品的合订：九本书（六本学术专著＋两本通俗读本＋一本科普专著）、十五篇工作论文，另附一篇卷首《中道周行集》。二十四件作品放在同一张桌子上，只为了看清一件事：
一个有限的东西，怎样一边保持自己的样子，一边还能改变？

核心主张。
看得见的世界背后都有一张看不见的账单，账单上只有三个价格——看得准、拼得上、传得下去，最多付得起两个；让出的那一个，就是每一卷要指认的东西。六个核心（看见、记住、跟上、选择、讲述、临界）不是六门学问，而是同一笔账的六个项目；它们不是并列的六个词，而是「起点—约束—枢纽—元层—门槛」的五层结构。

Abstract.
 This volume binds twenty-four items of one series — nine books (six monographs, two popular readers, one popular-science monograph), fifteen working papers, and a frontispiece in classical Chinese — to ask a single question: how does something finite keep its shape while still being able to change? The core claim is a bill with three prices — being precise, fitting together, carrying over — of which at most two can be paid. The six cores (distinction, memory, timing, choice, interpretation, criticality) are not six disciplines but six items on one bill, arranged as a five-layer structure: starting point, constraint, hinge, meta-layer, threshold.

, which breaks the “fundamental contradiction” between quantum mechanics and general relativity into 30 auditable items: 4 logical contradictions, 13 missing interfaces, 8 accounting conflicts, and 5 theorem boundaries; none of the four logical contradictions has been directly measured, and all four A-grade items sit on the “measured” or “proved theorem” side. The Language of Distinction
 and its new subtitle; series pages now use concept DOIs, and residual DOI misplacements are corrected. Fourth, the general and per-volume tables of contents have been re-measured and their page numbers refilled; paper 15 is included.

Discipline.
 Every item keeps its own evidence grades (A/B/C and 甲/乙/丙), falsifiability register and failure record. The collection does not flatten differences among items, nor does it turn the stage ledger into a “final conclusion”: the ledger audits registered propositions, not the world. Of the 126 propositions, 18 (14.3%) were killed by the authors' own data; the number is stated in the book.

Boundaries.
 This is a bound collection, not a new monograph; it offers coordinates and tools, not verdicts. Citations should follow the individual items (with their DOIs, appendices and registers). The frontispiece is a spiritual self-account and does not enter any citation count.
