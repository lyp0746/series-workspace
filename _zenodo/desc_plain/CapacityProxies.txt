Abstract.Three countable proxies for capacity are proposed and tested on existing data. Periodization slots support the intuition; coding slots are confounded with time; label slots run against it. Capacity is not unidirectional protection.

Background.This is item 17 of the series, following 优化的边界; it tests the series motif on the material named in the title.

Method and structure.18 sections, at most six appendices.

Results.(i) Periodisation slots: bins are monotone but the linear correlation is weak (r = −0.245), and gates cluster more than slots. (ii) Coding slots: the six-county direction is negative but undecidable—recoding at village level flips r from −0.36 to +0.12, exposing a level mix. (iii) Label slots: the four periods run the other way (larger inflow, higher pass rate), and three explanations cannot be separated. (iv) Cross-validation of the three proxies gives one positive, one negative, one zero. (v) Capacity is not one-directional protection; the three proxies are incommensurable.

Contributions.It contributes three things: it states the problem as checkable conditions; gives reproducible readings or structures; and publishes its boundaries, failures and retractions.
